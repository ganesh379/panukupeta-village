3. Platform Purpose & Village Development Fund

panukupeta.family is a dedicated non-commercial community initiative established to connect native residents, farmers, students, elders, and the global diaspora of Panukupeta Village (Seethanagaram Mandal, Parvathipuram Manyam District, Andhra Pradesh — 535546).

The Platform provides:

  • A verified digital family registry and demographic archive for Panukupeta households.
  • Village history, agriculture resources, and cultural festival documentation.
  • The Panukupeta Village Development & Welfare Fund (గ్రామ అభివృద్ధి నిధి), enabling residents and diaspora to voluntarily pool financial resources for public village welfare projects, including:
    • Safe RO drinking water plant installation and maintenance.
    • Solar street lighting and village sanitation improvement.
    • Merit scholarships, books, and sports equipment for local school youth.
    • Temple preservation, community hall maintenance, and Sankranti/Jatara Annadanam.
    • Emergency healthcare and disaster relief support for vulnerable village families.
